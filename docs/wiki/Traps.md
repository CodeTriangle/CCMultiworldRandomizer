The following is a list of traps available in CrossCode.

## Advisories

Certain traps have advisories, indicated by emojis,
with meanings indicating by the following table.
Please consider whether you want them before enabling them.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|--------|---|
| ♿     | Accessibility concern |
| 💢     | Extremely annoying, potentially time-consuming |

## The Traps

* **Artless Trap**: SP regens slower *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Bomb Trap**: Spawn Bombs *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Burglar's Rope Trap**: Gain 0 XP *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Clumsy Trap**: Can't dash for amount of time *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Combo Breaker Trap**: Your combat rank is set to D *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Death Trap**: You die *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Drunk Trap**: You can't aim straight *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Element Swap Trap**: Change element randomly *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Forgetfulness Trap**: Set XP to 0 *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Full Course Trap**: Get stuck in a long consumable animation *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Full Rotate Trap**: Rotate the camera to a random angle *(since 0.10.0)*
  * ♿ This trap can be visually disorienting.
    It may not be suitable for players with visual impairments.
* **Landmark Trap**: Forget all landmarks *(since 0.10.0)*
  * 💢 This trap will force you to rediscover all landmarks, which can take a long time.
* **Laser Of Doom Trap**: Vermillion Tower Laser follows you *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Naked Trap**: Remove all Equipment *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Overload Trap**: Immediately receive Elemental Overload *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Override Trap**: Circuits reset *(since 0.10.0)*
  * 💢 This trap will force you to recreate your entire build.
* **Poverty Trap**: Lose 20% of credits *(since 0.10.0)*
  * 💢 This trap can induce unnecessary grinding.
* **Rotate Trap**: Rotate the camera either 180 degrees *(since 0.10.0)*
  * ♿ This trap can be visually disorienting.
    It may not be suitable for players with visual impairments.
* **SP Trap**: Lose a lot of SP *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Slip Trap**: Fall off of edges instead of jumping *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Voidout Trap**: Falling into the void (water, sand, void, etc) kills you *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Winded Trap**: Reduce max dashes *(since 0.10.0)*
* **Zoom Out Trap**: Zoom in on Lea *(since 0.10.0)*
  * ♿ This trap can be visually disturbing and may result in flashing lights.
    It may not be suitable for players with photosensitive epilepsy or similar conditions.
* **Zoom Trap**: Zoom in on Lea *(since 0.10.0)*
